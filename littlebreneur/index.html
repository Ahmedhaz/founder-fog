Energy is a resource

Tired sellers make mistakes.

Selling and staying up late use energy. Rest and play bring it back. Below 40 the numbers wobble and blur, and change gets miscounted. At 70 or more, a third, wiser door opens on hard choices. It&#x27;s Founder Fog&#x27;s clarity, made for kids.

Questions

Before you start

What age is it for?

8 to 12, for children who can read short sentences on their own. Younger children enjoy it with a parent.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no account and nothing to buy.

Which currency does it use?

Plain coins with no country, so it works the same in Cairo, Riyadh or Dubai.

Can we use it in class?

Yes. A summer takes about 15 minutes and ends with a report card and Grandpa's ideas to talk about together.

Where is the data stored?

Only in your browser on this device. Clearing the browser's data starts the game over.

Is there a game for grown-ups?

Yes: Founder Fog, the startup game this one grew out of.

Ready for a summer of business?

Pick a business, set a goal, and see what Grandpa thinks.
