In the game, every week

Plan, sell, count, decide.

  1. Plan

    Pick a price, how many to make, and one activity: a poster, practice, homework or rest.

  2. Market day

    Customers arrive. Weather, price, quality and smiles decide how many.

  3. Count

    Coins in, coins spent, what is left. Making just enough means less waste.

  4. Decide

    A small dilemma with two fair answers. Then Grandpa shares one idea.

  5. Echo

    Some choices come back weeks later: “This is because of what you did in week 3.”

What children practise

Money skills, and the habits behind them.

Planning and patience

Counting before spending, saving for something that matters, and waiting when it is worth it.

Thinking of others

Listening when someone is unhappy, a fair price, and work done with care.

Balance

Rest, play and homework come first. A good plan makes room for all three.

Honesty and trying again

Owning a mistake, giving back extra change, and learning that a bad week is not the end.

Questions

Before you start

What age is it for?

8 to 12, for children who can read short sentences on their own. Younger children enjoy the book with a parent.

Are the game and the book free?

Yes, both. No ads, no account and nothing to buy. The PDF is free to print and share.

Will it make my child focus on money?

It does the opposite of chasing money. Every goal is something to share or enjoy, and the game rewards rest, homework and honesty as much as profit. The report card has stars for balance and honesty.

What if my child doesn't reach the goal?

That is part of learning. There are no harsh endings: Grandpa sums up what was learned and offers another summer. A good moment to say: mistakes are how we learn.

How much screen time is it?

About 15 minutes for a whole summer. Many families play one summer, then read a scene from the book away from the screen.

Do we need the book to play?

No. Each stands on its own. Together they work best: the story shows the ideas, the game lets children try them.

Which currency does it use?

Plain coins with no country, so it works the same in Cairo, Riyadh or Dubai.

How do I print the book?

Download the A5 PDF. Print it on A5, or on A4 at two pages per sheet, then fold.

Where is the data stored?

Only in the browser on this device. Clearing the browser's data starts the game over.

Ready for a summer of small, brave choices?

Start with the story at bedtime, or try the game together after school. Grandpa will be there either way.
