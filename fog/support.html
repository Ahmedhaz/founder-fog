Support

Questions, a bug, or ideas for the game? Email ahmed.abuelhaz@hotmail.com and we will reply within a few days. Please mention your device and the week you were in, if it is about a game.

Common questions

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no account, nothing to buy.

Where is my game saved? On your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

How do I switch language? On the title screen, tap EN or عربي. Your game is kept.

How do I turn off play statistics? In the game, open Trophies and switch them off.

Can I use it with a class or a cohort? Yes. Everyone can play the same year and compare report cards; the daily challenge gives everyone the same market and luck.
