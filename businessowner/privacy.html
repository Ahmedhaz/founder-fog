Business Owner

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026. This policy covers Business Owner, a game about running a small business, on the web (adam.ahmedhaz.com/owner) and in any app version.

We collect nothing

The game collects no personal data and sends nothing off the device: no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no chat, no leaderboard and no in-app purchases. It makes no network requests to anyone else; the fonts and pictures are part of the game.

What stays on the device

The player's first name, chosen picture, the game in progress, today's challenge score, trophies, Uncle Salah's notebook and the Owner's Playbook pages are saved only on the device, so the game can continue. Deleting the app, or clearing the browser's data, removes them.

Training programmes

Incubators, banks and training programmes that use the game in a session receive no data about participants from it. Any score a participant shares is shared by them, by their own choice.

Contact

Questions from players or programmes: ahmed.abuelhaz@hotmail.com · support.
