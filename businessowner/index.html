Every month

Plan, open, count, decide.

  1. Plan

    Set the price, the size of the team, and one focus: marketing, systems, collecting money or rest.

  2. The month

    Customers come. The season, your price, your quality and your reputation decide how many.

  3. Count

    Profit on one side, cash on the other: what's not paid yet, the loan, what came in from before.

  4. Decide

    A real dilemma: a cousin who needs a job, a big client who pays in 90 days. Then Salah leaves a note.

  5. Echo

    Some choices come back months later: “This is because of what you did in month 5.”

You are part of the business

Your energy is on the balance sheet.

Every month you work, energy drops. Below 40, the numbers blur and mistakes happen. At 70 or more, a smarter option opens in every dilemma. At 0, you spend a month in bed, and the business runs without you, ready or not. Systems and a good manager are how an owner gets time back.

Questions

Before you start

Who is it for?

People thinking about opening a business, and owners who already run one. For teenagers there is Teenpreneur, for children The Little Entrepreneur, and for founders of startups, Bousla.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no account and nothing to buy.

How realistic are the numbers?

Simplified, but built on how small businesses really behave: rent and salaries before the first customer, clients who pay months later, food that spoils, parcels that come back, and quiet and busy seasons like Ramadan and the summer.

How long is a game?

About 30 minutes for two years, and it saves as you go. Today's challenge is 12 months.

Which currency does it use?

Dollars, so the game works the same everywhere.

Can we use it in our programme?

Yes, freely. Nothing to install and nothing to sign. We would love to hear how it went.

Where is the data stored?

Only on this device. Clearing the browser's data starts the game over.
