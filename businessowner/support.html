Common questions

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no account, nothing to buy.

Who is it for? People thinking about opening a business, and owners who already run one.

Where is the game saved? On the device only. Deleting the app, or clearing the browser's data, removes it.

How do I switch language? Tap العربية or English at the top of the game screen. The game in progress is kept.

What is today's challenge? A 12-month game with the same business, start and events for everyone who plays that day. A new one starts every day.

Can we use it in a training programme? Yes, freely. Write to us if you would like to tell us how it went.

هل يمكن استخدامها في برنامج تدريب؟ نعم، مجانًا. ويسعدنا أن نعرف كيف كانت التجربة.
