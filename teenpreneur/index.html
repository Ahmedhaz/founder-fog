In the game, every week

Plan, work, count, decide.

  1. Plan

    Set a price, how many to prepare, and one extra thing: promote, learn, study or rest.

  2. Work week

    Customers come. The week, your price, your quality and your reputation decide how many.

  3. Count

    Coins in, coins spent, what is left. Preparing just enough means less waste.

  4. Decide

    A real dilemma: a fake payment, a friend who wants it free, a cruel comment. Then Nour leaves a note.

  5. Echo

    Some choices come back weeks later: “This is because of what you did in week 3.”

Every week without studying lets grades slide, faster in exam season. Below 40, your parents pause the business for a week. Three times a year the results arrive. Studying is not a punishment here: it is a resource, like energy and money.

Questions

Before you start

What age is it for?

13 to 17. For younger children there is The Little Entrepreneur, and for adults starting a company, Founder Fog.

Is it free?

The game is free, with no ads, no account and nothing to buy. Chapter 1 of the book is free to read online and to download. The full book is in print.

Won't it make my teen care more about money than school?

It does the opposite. Grades are a meter, exam seasons pause the business if grades drop, and the year report rewards school, rest and integrity as much as profit.

Is it safe?

Nothing is collected and there is nothing online in the game: no chat, no profiles, no other players. The book and the game both teach how to spot scams and stay safe with strangers.

How long is a school year?

About 40 minutes. It saves as you go, so it is easy to play a few weeks at a time.

Do I need the book to play?

No. Each stands on its own. The story shows the ideas, and the game lets you try them.

Which currency does it use?

The game uses plain coins, so it works the same everywhere. The book's story uses pounds, with real prices.

Where is the data stored?

Only on this device. Clearing the browser's data starts the game over.

Ready for your first real customer?

Read chapter 1 tonight, or start a school year now. Aunt Nour has notes either way.
