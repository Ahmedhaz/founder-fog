Common questions

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no account, nothing to buy.

What age is it for? 13 to 17.

Where is the game saved? On the device only. Deleting the app removes it.

How do I switch language? Tap العربية or English at the bottom of the game screen. The school year is kept.

Is there a book? Yes, a picture book that follows Laila through one school year. Read chapter 1 online.

أسئلة شائعة
