Teenpreneur

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026. This policy covers Teenpreneur, a game for ages 13 to 17, on the web (adam.ahmedhaz.com/teen) and in the iPhone app.

We collect nothing

The game collects no personal data and sends nothing off the device: no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no chat, no leaderboard and no in-app purchases. It makes no network requests at all; the fonts and pictures are part of the app.

What stays on the device

The player's first name, chosen picture, school years in progress and the notebook of collected notes are saved only on the device, so the game can continue. Deleting the app, or clearing the browser's data, removes them.

Young people

The game is made for teenagers. Because nothing is collected, no personal information about a player ever reaches us or anyone else.

Contact

Questions from players, parents or teachers: ahmed.abuelhaz@hotmail.com · support.
